2. Information the App Handles

The app accesses the camera to display a preview, take photos, and record videos. It accesses the microphone when needed to include sound in a video recording. Access to selected or saved media allows the app to perform the photo and video functions you choose.

Your captured photos, videos, and audio are saved locally. The app does not automatically upload this media to our servers. When you share media, the destination app or service receives the content you select. A cloud backup service enabled on your device may separately back up your media.

Permissions

The permissions requested depend on your Android version and the feature you use. These can include camera access, microphone access for video sound, and access to photos, videos, or storage for selecting and saving media.

You can review or withdraw permissions in Android settings. A feature that depends on a withdrawn permission may become unavailable.

Technical, usage, and advertising information

The Google services integrated into the app may process information such as device model, operating-system version, app version, language, app interactions, advertising interactions, diagnostic information, IP address, and advertising or app-installation identifiers. The information processed depends on the integrated services, their configuration, and applicable consent choices.

An IP address may be used by advertising services to estimate your general location. This is separate from granting the app permission to access your device’s precise location.

Information you send for support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments you choose to include. You may also provide device information or screenshots to help explain a problem. Please include only the information needed to handle your request.
